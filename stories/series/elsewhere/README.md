# Elsewhere

Alternate-universe story series featuring Ninja and Hex.

Pilot: **The Photograph**. Hex's past with the Directorate and Operation Glasshouse leads to his disappearance and a new life in Bellwether; Ninja's life is affected by the operation. The pilot ends just as they first meet eyes at the Crooked Lantern.

Episodes live in `episodes/episode-001/`, etc. Do not treat a draft as a completed recording or published Ohana episode.
